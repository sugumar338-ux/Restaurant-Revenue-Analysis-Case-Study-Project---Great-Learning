# Restaurant Revenue Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing restaurant business data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, and key factors affecting revenue.

The main objective is to identify data-driven opportunities to **increase restaurant revenue without expanding the restaurant**.

## Business Problem

The restaurant wants to improve its revenue using its existing resources and operations. The analysis helps identify which products, categories, customer segments, and sales patterns contribute most to revenue and where improvements can be made.

## Objectives

* Analyze restaurant sales and revenue performance.
* Identify high-performing and low-performing products or categories.
* Understand customer and sales patterns.
* Analyze factors affecting restaurant revenue.
* Identify opportunities to improve revenue without expansion.
* Create meaningful visualizations and dashboards.
* Provide actionable business recommendations.

## Key Business Questions

* Which products or categories generate the most revenue?
* Which products have lower performance?
* What are the major revenue drivers?
* Which customer or sales patterns can be leveraged?
* Where are opportunities to improve existing sales?
* How can revenue be increased without expanding the restaurant?

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the restaurant can consider strategies such as:

* Promoting high-performing products.
* Improving sales of profitable products.
* Introducing targeted offers during low-demand periods.
* Focusing marketing efforts on valuable customer segments.
* Optimizing product and menu placement.
* Using customer and sales data for targeted promotions.
* Improving operational efficiency during peak periods.
